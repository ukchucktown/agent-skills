Title: Patient self-service

As a patient, I want to do all of my clinic admin online, end to end, including booking, cancelling and rescheduling appointments, requesting repeat prescriptions, paying and disputing bills, and messaging my clinician, so that I never need to phone the clinic.

Acceptance criteria:
- Given I am signed in, then I can complete every one of these tasks without calling the clinic.
- Given I am not signed in, then I am asked to sign in first.
