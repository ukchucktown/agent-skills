Title: Online appointment cancellation

Appointments can be cancelled online so that slots are freed for other people and missed-appointment fees are avoided.

Acceptance criteria:
- Given an appointment that starts more than 24 hours from now, when it is cancelled, then it shows as cancelled and the slot shows as available to book.
- Given an appointment that starts in less than 24 hours, when cancellation is attempted, then a message says online cancellation is closed and shows the clinic phone number.
- Given the cancellation cannot be saved, then the appointment still shows as booked.

Notes: The 24-hour limit is clinic policy (Patient Handbook, section 4).
