Title: Pay the missed-appointment fee with my saved card

As a patient, I want to pay a missed-appointment fee with the card saved on my profile so that I can clear the fee in one step and book again.

Acceptance criteria:
- Given I owe a missed-appointment fee and have a saved card, when I choose to pay, then I see the amount, and after I confirm, the fee shows as paid and I receive a receipt by email.
- Given the card is declined, when I confirm, then I see that the payment failed and the fee still shows as owed.
- Given I have no saved card, then I do not see the one-step option.
- Given I owe no fee, then no payment option appears.
