Title: Recent patient activity

As a receptionist, I want to see recent activity for a patient when I open their record, so that I can answer their questions on the phone without searching several screens.

Acceptance criteria:
- Given a patient with activity, when I open their record, then the recent activity is listed, newest first.
- Given a patient with no activity, when I open their record, then I see that there is no recent activity.
- Given I am signed in as a patient, then I do not see other patients' records.
