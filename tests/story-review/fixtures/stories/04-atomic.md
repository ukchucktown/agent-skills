Title: Reschedule and update insurance

As a patient, I want to move an upcoming appointment to a different time and also update the insurance details on my profile, so that my visit fits my week and my claim goes to the right insurer.

Acceptance criteria:
- Given I have an appointment more than 24 hours away, when I pick a different free slot, then my appointment list shows the new time and the old slot shows as available.
- Given the slot I pick is taken before I confirm, then I see that it is no longer free and my original appointment is unchanged.
- Given I enter a new insurer and policy number, when I save, then my profile shows the new details.
- Given I leave the policy number empty, when I save, then I see that a policy number is required and my old details are unchanged.
