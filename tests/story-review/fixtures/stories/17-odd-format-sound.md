[Patient Portal] PP-142 * Trial visit reminders

As an R&D Nurse & Trial Coordinator, I want to send a reminder to every participant who has a trial visit tomorrow so that fewer participants miss a visit and the trial keeps its schedule.

Acceptance criteria:
- Given three participants have a visit tomorrow, when I choose "send reminders", then each of the three receives one reminder with the visit time and location, and I see a count of three sent.
- Given a participant has no contact method on file, when I send reminders, then that participant is listed as not reached and the others are still sent.
- Given nobody has a visit tomorrow, when I choose "send reminders", then I see that there is nobody to remind and nothing is sent.
- Given I am signed in as a receptionist, then the option to send trial reminders is not offered.
