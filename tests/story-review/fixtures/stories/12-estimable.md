Title: Show my appointment in the national health record app

As a patient, I want an appointment that I book with the clinic to appear in the national health record app, so that I see all of my healthcare appointments in one place.

Acceptance criteria:
- Given I book an appointment and have linked my national health record, when the booking is confirmed, then the appointment appears in the national app with its date, time, and clinic name.
- Given I cancel that appointment, then it no longer appears in the national app.
- Given I have not linked my national health record, then booking works as it does today and nothing is sent.
- Given the national service does not accept the appointment, then my clinic booking still stands and the clinic sees that the appointment was not shared.

Notes: TBD - we have not confirmed whether the national record service accepts appointments written by third-party clinics at all, or only reads. The whole approach depends on the answer. Nobody on the team has used this service.
