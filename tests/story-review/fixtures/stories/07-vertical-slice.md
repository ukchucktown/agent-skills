Title: Cancel appointment screen

As a patient, I want the cancel-appointment screen to exist so that I can see the cancellation form for an upcoming appointment.

Acceptance criteria:
- Given I have an upcoming appointment, when I choose cancel, then I see a form that shows the appointment date, time, and clinician, and a confirm option.
- Given I press confirm, then nothing is saved yet. Saving the cancellation and freeing the slot belong to the back-end story planned for the following iteration.
- Given I am not signed in, then I am asked to sign in before the form appears.
