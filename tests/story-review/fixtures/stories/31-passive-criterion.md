Title: Pay the missed-appointment fee online

As a patient, I want to pay a missed-appointment fee online by card so that I can clear the fee and book again without a phone call.

Acceptance criteria:
- Given I owe a missed-appointment fee, when I pay by card and the payment succeeds, then the fee shows as paid and a receipt is emailed to me.
- Given the card is declined, when I confirm, then I see that the payment failed and the fee still shows as owed.
- Given I owe no fee, then no payment option appears.
- Given I am not signed in, then I am asked to sign in before I see any fee.

Notes: Card payments use the payment page that the portal already has for prescription charges.
