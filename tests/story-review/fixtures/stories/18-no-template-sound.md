Cancel an upcoming appointment online

Patients can cancel an upcoming appointment online. This frees the slot for someone else and the patient avoids the missed-appointment fee.

Done when:
- A patient who cancels more than 24 hours before the start sees the appointment as cancelled, and the slot shows as available to book.
- A patient who tries to cancel less than 24 hours before the start sees that online cancellation is closed, with the clinic phone number.
- A person who is not signed in is asked to sign in before anything changes.
- If the cancellation cannot be saved, the patient sees that the appointment is still booked.

The 24-hour limit is clinic policy (Patient Handbook, section 4).
