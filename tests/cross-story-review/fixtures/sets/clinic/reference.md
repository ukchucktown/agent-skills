# Clinic online appointments: requirements, version 3 of 2024-02-01

| Id | Requirement |
|---|---|
| R1 | A patient can book a free slot with a clinician online. |
| R2 | A patient can see the upcoming and the past appointments. |
| R3 | A patient can cancel an appointment online until 24 hours before its start. Later, only the clinic can cancel it. |
| R4 | A patient with an appointment can join a waitlist and is offered an earlier slot. |
| R5 | A patient can move an appointment to another free slot. |
| R6 | A patient can add an appointment to a calendar with a calendar file. |
| R7 | A patient receives a reminder email one day before an appointment. |
| R8 | Later release, not in this set: a patient can pay the fee for a missed appointment online. |
| R9 | A receptionist can cancel an appointment for a patient at the desk. |
| R10 | The booking page shows no slots on clinic closure days. |
