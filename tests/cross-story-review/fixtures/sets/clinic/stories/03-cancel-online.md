Key: CL-3
Title: Cancel an upcoming appointment online

As a patient, I want to cancel an upcoming appointment online so that the slot is free for someone else.

Acceptance criteria:
- Given my appointment starts 24 hours or more from now, when I cancel it, then my appointment list shows it as cancelled and the slot is free to book.
- Given my appointment starts in less than 24 hours, when I try to cancel it, then I see that online cancellation is closed and I see the clinic phone number.

Notes: Depends on CL-1 and CL-2.
