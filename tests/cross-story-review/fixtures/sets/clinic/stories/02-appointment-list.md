Key: CL-2
Title: See my appointments

As a patient, I want to see my upcoming and past appointments so that I know when to come to the clinic.

Acceptance criteria:
- Given I have appointments, when I open my appointment list, then I see each appointment with its date, time, clinician, and state.
- Given I have no appointments, when I open my appointment list, then I see that I have none and a link to book one.

Notes: Depends on CL-1, which creates the appointments.
