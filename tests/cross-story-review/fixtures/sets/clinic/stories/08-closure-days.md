Key: CL-8
Title: No booking on clinic closure days

As a patient, I want the booking page to hide the clinic closure days so that I do not select a day when the clinic is closed.

Acceptance criteria:
- Given a day is a clinic closure day, when I look for a slot, then that day shows no slots and the text "The clinic is closed on this day".
- Given a closure day is added after I booked, when the day is saved, then I receive an email that asks me to book again.

Notes: Depends on CL-1.
