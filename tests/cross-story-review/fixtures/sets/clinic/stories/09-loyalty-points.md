Key: CL-9
Title: Points for attended appointments

As a patient, I want to collect one point for each appointment that I attend so that I get a discount on a later treatment.

Acceptance criteria:
- Given I attended an appointment, when the clinician closes it, then my account shows one more point.
- Given I have ten points, when I book a treatment, then I can use the points for a discount of 10 percent.

Notes: Depends on CL-1.
