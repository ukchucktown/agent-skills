Key: CL-7
Title: Withdraw a booking

As a patient, I want to withdraw a booking that I can no longer attend so that another patient can use the time.

Acceptance criteria:
- Given I have a booking in the future, when I withdraw it, then it shows as withdrawn in my list and the time is open for other patients.
- Given the booking is too close to its start, when I try to withdraw it, then I am told to call the clinic.
