Key: CL-5
Title: Move a visit to another time

As a patient, I want to move a visit to another time so that I keep my place with the same clinician.

Acceptance criteria:
- Given my visit starts 2 hours or more from now, when I select a new free slot, then my visit moves to that slot and the old slot is free.
- Given my visit starts 2 hours or more from now, when I cancel it online, then the visit is cancelled and the slot is free.
- Given my visit starts in less than 2 hours, when I try to move it, then I see the clinic phone number.

Notes: Depends on CL-1.
