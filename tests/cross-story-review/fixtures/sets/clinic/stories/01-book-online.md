Key: CL-1
Title: Book an appointment online

As a patient, I want to book an appointment online so that I do not have to call the clinic.

Acceptance criteria:
- Given a clinician has a free slot, when I select it and confirm, then my appointment list shows the appointment and the slot is no longer free.
- Given two patients select the same slot, when both confirm, then one patient gets the appointment and the other sees that the slot is taken.
- Given I am not signed in, when I select a slot, then I am asked to sign in before anything is saved.
