Key: CL-4
Title: Join the waitlist for an earlier slot

As a patient with an appointment, I want to join a waitlist so that I am offered an earlier slot when one becomes free.

Acceptance criteria:
- Given I have an upcoming appointment, when I join the waitlist, then my appointment list shows that I am on the waitlist.
- Given a slot before my appointment becomes free, when I am first on the waitlist, then I receive an offer for that slot.
- Given I accept the offer, when I confirm, then my appointment moves to the earlier slot and my old slot is free.

Notes: Depends on CL-3, because a cancelled appointment is what frees a slot.
