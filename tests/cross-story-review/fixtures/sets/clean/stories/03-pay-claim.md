Key: EX-3
Title: Pay approved expense claims

As a finance clerk, I want a list of approved expense claims so that I can pay them with the next salary run.

Acceptance criteria:
- Given claims have the state "approved", when I open the payment list, then I see each one with the employee and the amount.
- Given I mark a claim as paid, when I confirm, then it has the state "paid" and leaves the list.

Notes: Depends on EX-2.
