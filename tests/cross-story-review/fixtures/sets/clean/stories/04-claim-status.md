Key: EX-4
Title: See the state of my expense claims

As an employee, I want to see the state of each expense claim so that I know when I am paid.

Acceptance criteria:
- Given I have claims, when I open my claims, then I see each one with its amount and its state.
- Given a claim has the state "rejected", when I open it, then I see the reason of the manager.

Notes: Depends on EX-1 and EX-2. The state "paid" comes from EX-3.
