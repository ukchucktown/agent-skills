# Notice

## What this skill contains

Original work. The criteria, the procedure, the severity levels, the verdict
rule, the report format, and the fixture stories are our own wording.

The criteria draw on the published sources listed in
`references/criteria.md`. A citation such as `Cohn Ch. 7 "Slice the Cake"` or
`QUS "Atomic"` names the chapter, section heading, or criterion where a reader
can find the idea. The citations are references. They are not quotations.

## What this skill does not contain

No text from any source. The books, articles, and the paper belong to their
authors and publishers and live outside this repository.

## How far each citation was checked

- Wake, Jeffries, North, Lawrence and Green: read from the published web pages.
- Lucassen and others (QUS): read from the published paper.
- Cohn: the section headings of Chapters 2, 6, 7, and 14 are exact. The meaning
  attached to each heading comes from a secondhand summary of the book, and
  nobody checked it against the book text. Treat a Cohn citation as a
  pointer to the right section, not as a statement of what the section says.
